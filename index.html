03 / THE FULL EXPERIENCE

Made for your terminal.
Built to go further.

The original Enigmatic Player is a Rust-powered desktop music player with real audio playback, a real-time spectrum visualizer, local playlists and online music sources.

  • Real audio playback powered by Rust
  • YouTube, SoundCloud and Bandcamp support via yt-dlp
  • 33 themes, live reverb and playback speed control
  • Local music library and saved playlists
  • Lightweight terminal interface
